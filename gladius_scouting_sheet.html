Gladius Task Force — Ultramarines
Space Marines · Strike Force 2000pts · Scouting Sheet
Army Rule
Oath of Moment: Re-roll hits · +1 to wound (pure Ultramarines)
Detachment Rule — Combat Doctrines (one per game each, chosen start of Command phase)
Devastator
Advance + Shoot. Early repositioning without losing firepower. Re-roll Advance rolls for Fire Discipline bearer's unit.
⚠ Tactical
Fall Back + Shoot + Charge. Most dangerous — Victrix/Calgar can disengage and still charge. Squad Tactics reactive move becomes full 6" (not D6").
Assault
Advance + Charge. Extends threat range dramatically. Jump Pack AIs with 12" move + D6 advance + 2D6 charge can threaten 24"+ zones.
Stratagems
2CP
Fight on Death Only in Death Does Duty End
Fight phase, after enemy selects targets. Full fight on death — NO roll per model. Every model not yet fought fires back before removal. No 4+. No roll. All of them. Critical threat on Victrix and Calgar. Do not charge unless you can clear the entire unit.
1CP
Reactive Move Squad Tactics
Opponent's Movement phase after any enemy Normal/Advance/Fall Back move. One Infantry/Mounted within 9" of that unit makes a Normal move up to D6", or up to 6" if under Tactical Doctrine. Triggers on YOUR moves — any model you move near a Victrix unit may give them a free reposition or closer surge setup.
1CP
Adaptive Strategy
Command phase. Swap one unit into any doctrine independently of the army's active doctrine. Lets them put Victrix in Assault while army is in Tactical, etc.
1CP
Storm of Fire
Shooting phase. Target unit gains Ignores Cover. Under Devastator: also AP-1. Core combo with Oath re-rolls and +1 wound. Primary strat on Lancers and Ballistus.
1CP
Honour the Chapter
Fight phase. Unit gains Lance on melee weapons. Under Assault: also AP-1. Victrix Master-crafted Power Swords hit AP-3 with this active.
1CP
Armour of Contempt
Shooting or Fight phase after enemy selects targets. Worsen AP of all incoming attacks by 1. Universal defensive staple in every SM detachment.
CP Engine
Master Tactician — Marneus Calgar
+1 CP at start of every Command phase while on the battlefield. Expect 5–8 bonus CP across a game. They can fund Fight on Death + Armour of Contempt + Squad Tactics in the same turn.
Enhancement In List
Fire Discipline — Lieutenant (25pts)
Sustained Hits 1 on all ranged weapons in the bearer's unit. Under Devastator: re-roll Advance rolls. Likely on Hellblasters — 10 plasma incinerators fishing 6s for extra hits. With Oath re-rolls expect very high output.
Battleline Army Passive — Auspex Scan
After any Adeptus Astartes Battleline unit (Intercessors) attacks, select one enemy unit it hit — that unit is Auspex Scanned until end of turn. All other Adeptus Astartes models re-roll Hit rolls of 1 against it. Intercessors shoot first → Lancers, Hellblasters, Dreadnoughts all get free hit re-roll buffing on the same target.
Marneus Calgar + Victrix Honour Guard (attached)
Advance + Charge Fall Back + Shoot + Charge Fight on Death OC 2/model
Inspiring Leader (Calgar): the unit can Advance+Charge OR Fall Back+Shoot+Charge — always on, not doctrine-dependent.
Ultramarines Honour Guard: while a Captain or Chapter Master leads this unit, -1 to Wound on all attacks targeting it. Calgar satisfies this.
Glory of Ultramar (Surge): in your Shooting phase, whenever you destroy any model in this unit, the whole unit makes a D6" move toward nearest enemy — free, no CP, auto-triggers. Can enter Engagement Range. Do NOT chip a model off to weaken them — you move them closer.
Honour Guard of Macragge: Calgar gains Feel No Pain 4+ while the unit contains any Victrix models.
Master Tactician: +1 CP per turn.
Victrix Honour Guard — Standalone Units (x2)
OC 2/model Surge D6"
Unit with Chapter Ancient (Banner of Macragge): +1 OC to all models in unit → OC 3 per model = 18 total OC on a 6-model unit. Near-impossible to contest.
Unit with Chapter Champion (Blades of Honour): +1 to Advance and Charge rolls. Heroic Intervention costs 0 CP on this unit — free counter-charge in your Fight phase.
Glory of Ultramar: same D6" auto-surge as Calgar's unit — applies to all three Victrix units. Multiple surge triggers possible in one phase.
No Captain/Chapter Master attached → no -1 to Wound on standalone units. More killable through shooting.
Hellblaster Squad (10 models + Lieutenant)
Shoot on Death 3+ Hazardous Sustained Hits 1
For the Chapter! (3+): each time a model is destroyed for ANY reason including a failed Hazardous test — roll D6, on 3+ it shoots once more before removal. That final shot's Hazardous test is automatically passed. A model that blows itself up on a 1 can shoot twice in the same phase (~67% chance).
Hazardous allocation: 1s must be allocated to Character models first. While the Lieutenant is alive he absorbs overcharge deaths, protecting rank-and-file. Once he dies every 1 risks a free extra shot.
10 models overcharging with Sustained Hits 1 + Oath re-rolls + potential revenge shots = extremely high output. Prioritise removing the Lieutenant to shut off the Hazardous shield.
Gladiator Lancers (x2)
Built-in Re-rolls
Aquilon Optics (datasheet, free): each time this model shoots, re-roll one Hit roll, one Wound roll, and one Damage roll. Both Lancers have this.
Stacked with Oath of Moment re-roll hits + +1 wound, and Storm of Fire (Ignores Cover, AP-1), these are extremely reliable anti-tank. Very hard to go cold on an important shot.
Lancer laser destroyer: high S, high AP, multi-damage. Your vehicles and monsters are the primary targets.
Ballistus Dreadnought
Built-in Re-rolls
Ballistus Strike (datasheet, free): each time it makes a ranged attack targeting a unit not Below Half-strength, re-roll the Hit roll. Applies to lascannon and missile launcher attacks against healthy targets.
Lascannon + missile launcher covers multiple profiles. With Oath, Storm of Fire, and Ballistus Strike it has very consistent output on priority targets at full health.
Impulsor
Assault Vehicle 5+ Invuln
Assault Vehicle: passengers can disembark after the Impulsor has Advanced. They count as having made a Normal move and cannot charge that turn, but can shoot. Allows aggressive repositioning without completely sacrificing the passenger unit's shooting phase.
Shield Dome: 5+ invulnerable save on the Impulsor itself (not passengers). Keeps it alive through light fire to deliver its cargo.
Incursor Squad
Infiltrate
Infiltrators: deploy anywhere on the battlefield more than 9" from enemy deployment zone AND all enemy models. Cannot charge turn 1. Use to block your Infiltrate/Deep Strike corridors from being used against you pre-game.
Multi-spectrum Array: after shooting, pick one enemy unit hit — all other friendly Adeptus Astartes attacks against it get +1 to Hit until end of phase. Force multiplier when combo'd with Lancers/Hellblasters on same target.
Haywire Mine (once per battle): 3" range, 2+ → D3 mortal wounds or 2D3 vs Vehicles.
Scout Squad + Assault Intercessors w/ Jump Packs
Scout 6" Advance + Charge Fly
Scout Squad — Scouts 6": pre-game move up to 6" (must end 9"+ from all enemies). Board control and blocking opponent Infiltrate corridors. Scout only — no Infiltrate.
Assault Intercessors w/ Jump Packs — Fly: 12" move. Under Assault Doctrine via Adaptive Strategy: advance D6" then charge 2D6". Total threat range can exceed 24" in a single activation. Hard to screen against reliably.
Captain + Lieutenant (Power Fists)
Epic Challenge Threat Precision Source
Captain with Power Fist is a Character with significant melee output — S×2, AP-3, D3+ damage across multiple attacks. Potential total damage output exceeds 20, making them an Epic Challenge threat to your Characters (2CP, all attacks gain Precision until end of phase).
Know which unit each Captain and Lieutenant is leading — they are force multipliers for that unit and personal threats to any of your attached Characters.
⚠ Key Tactical Alerts
Fight on Death — 2CP, no roll, full unit. With Calgar generating +1 CP/turn, they can run this every time it matters. Never commit a large melee unit into Victrix or Calgar's brick unless you can kill every last model, or you are prepared to eat the entire unit's attacks back.
Surge is free and automatic. Triggers in your Shooting phase each time you kill even one Victrix model. Do not chip damage Victrix to weaken before charging — you move the whole unit D6" closer mid-phase. Either commit to killing the unit entirely when shooting, or don't shoot them at all.
0 CP Heroic Intervention on the Chapter Champion unit. That specific Victrix unit (with Blades of Honour) can counter-charge in your Fight phase at zero cost. Budget for this and sequence your fights accordingly.
Squad Tactics 9" trigger. Any of your models ending a Normal, Advance, or Fall Back move within 9" of an eligible Victrix/Infantry unit can give them a free D6" (or 6" in Tactical Doctrine) reactive move. They can surge closer or retreat behind cover. Mentally map the 9" bubble around all Victrix units before you move.
Auspex Scan force multiplier. If Intercessors shoot your unit first in a phase, every subsequent Adeptus Astartes attack against that unit re-rolls 1s to hit in addition to individual unit re-rolls. Stack Oath of Moment on top. Don't let Intercessors get a free hit-buffer shot on your key units before the Lancers activate.
Assault Vehicle disembark after Advance. The Impulsor can advance and still deploy its passengers to shoot (not charge). Track what's inside — a Captain or buffed unit disembarking mid-advance can appear much further up the board than expected.
Multiple combats likely — watch CP for Counter-Offensive interruption. With three Victrix bricks plus Calgar potentially engaged simultaneously, both players may want to interrupt fight phase order. Monitor opponent's CP total before committing fight sequence.

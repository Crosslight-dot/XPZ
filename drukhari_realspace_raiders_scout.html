Army Rule — Power from Pain

Pain Token Economy

Drukhari gain Pain tokens: +1 each Command phase, +1 each time an enemy unit is destroyed, +1 each time an enemy fails a Battle-shock test. Tokens are spent to Empower a unit, triggering its Pain ability for that phase. Attached units: spending 1 token activates ALL leaders' and bodyguard Pain abilities at once — no extra cost per model.

REALSPACE RAIDERS BONUS: This army starts the game with bonus tokens from the Detachment Rule (see below) — potentially 6–7 tokens before Turn 1 begins.

⚠ Key Tactical Alerts

CP Economy & Malys Vect Aura

  • VECT AURA (12"): Lady Malys imposes +1 CP cost on every Stratagem your opponent uses against units within 12" of her. Keep her near your key units. Counter-Offensive costs 3CP, Heroic Intervention costs 2CP, Fighting Shadows costs 2CP in her bubble. Plan your Strat usage around when she's alive.
  • CP generation: Standard 1CP/turn Command phase. Labyrinthine Cunning enhancement is NOT in this list — no bonus CP generation beyond base. Their Pain token pool is the primary resource, not CP.
  • Interruption watch: With Incubi + Wyches frequently in combat simultaneously, watch both players' CP pools — Counter-Offensive (2CP) is live every fight phase. Cronos' Spirit Harvest feeds pain tokens to keep Empowering through the game.

Deployment & Pre-game Movement

  • Precognisant (Lady Malys): After armies are deployed, opponent redeploies up to 3 Drukhari units, optionally placing them in Strategic Reserves. This happens AFTER you finish deploying — plan your deployment order to minimise wasted effort.
  • Mandrakes BOTH Infiltrate AND Deep Strike: They can forward deploy within 12" of own DZ, 9"+ from enemies via Infiltrate. OR they can start in Reserves and Deep Strike. AND they can use Fade Away mid-game to disappear back to SR and re-appear anywhere next turn (costs 1 Pain token). This gives them total board flexibility.
  • Scourges Deep Strike: They can arrive T1 normally at 9"+, or when Empowered (spending a Pain token) at 6" — but cannot charge if they do. Rapid Ingress (1CP core) is available if opponent has units coming off the board.

Advance+Charge & Mobility Threats

  • Wyches + Lelith — Advance+Charge: Their Pain ability (spend 1 token at start of Charge phase) enables Advance AND Charge. Combine with Eager for the Kill (1CP, flat +6") for a 14" Advance + 2D6" Charge from up to 16"+ away on Turn 1. Fights First on that unit.
  • Reavers — 24" Advance: When Empowered (Pain token), Reavers advance a flat 8" added to 16" Move = 24" movement. Can fly over enemies and deal mortal wounds on 4+ per model. 6-man squads have OC2 per model — high objective threat.
  • Hellions — Reactive D6" move (9" trigger): When any enemy unit moves to within 9" of Hellions, Hellions make a reactive move of D6". This is NOT their full Move characteristic — purely D6". Use to dodge charges or slip off objectives. Note: Hellions lost Fall Back+Charge in this codex.

Shoot & Scoot / Fall Back Abilities

  • Scourges (own built-in): End of your Shooting phase, Scourges make a Normal move. This is their own ability — Raid and Fade cannot target them. They naturally relocate every turn after shooting.
  • Raid and Fade (1CP, Strat): Up to two Kabalite Warriors units (or one other non-Scourge/non-Aircraft) make a Normal move of up to 6" at the end of your Shooting phase. Cannot charge after. Can embark into a nearby Raider.
  • Drukhari army-wide Fall Back rule: The Archon datasheet (not present here) normally grants a unit Fall Back+Shoot+Charge. Lady Malys as ARCHON keyword: confirm whether she grants Fall Back and Charge to her attached unit — she is classified as ARCHON keyword. Check datasheet specifics; if she grants Feigned Weakness this is a potent threat vector.

Fight Phase Priority Threats

  • Drazhar: 2+/4++ save, re-rolls hits vs Characters, [TWIN-LINKED] wounds, Devastating Wounds option. Leading Incubi unit = 6" Consolidate. He represents the primary Epic Challenge threat in this list — likely potential damage output against characters exceeds 20 if Twin-Linked wounds land. Priority counter-plan needed.
  • Lelith Hesperax: [PRECISION] with Sustained Hits 2 + Anti-Infantry 2+. Her bodyguard Wyches get Fights First. D1 damage limits lethality vs multi-wound targets, but she will shred infantry Characters efficiently with precision targeting. Once per game: spikes to 12 attacks.
  • Incubi (Drazhar unit): Force Battle-shock on all enemy units in Engagement Range at Fight phase start. If Empowered, melee vs INFANTRY gains [DEVASTATING WOUNDS]. +1 to Hit vs Battle-shocked (synergy). Dark Harvest (1CP) adds Lethal Hits on top.
  • Fight on Death: No native fight-on-death ability confirmed in this detachment or these units. Monitor if any future strat/ability enables it, but it is not present in this list at current data.

Pain Token Acceleration — Mid/Late Game

  • 3× Cronos = token factory: Each Cronos generates 1 Pain token per enemy model destroyed in Fight phase. With 3 Cronos active mid-game, any fight round with multiple kills generates 3+ tokens. Combined with Command phase generation + kill tokens, this army can be flush with tokens by Turn 3.
  • Haemonculus Pain Adept: +1 token on 4+ each Command phase. At 5 rounds = expected 2–3 bonus tokens across the game.
  • Insensible to Pain (2CP): Key strat to protect Cronos or Wracks on objectives from burst damage. Reduces all incoming damage by 1. Malys Vect Aura makes the opponent pay 3CP if they use a strat targeting a unit within 12" of her — this effectively taxes finish-off stratagems.

Objective Control Notes

  • Kabalite Warriors OC2 + Sticky: 10 Kabalites = 20 OC, and leave objectives sticky. A Raider carrying Kabalites also gains Sticky benefit while they are embarked.
  • Reavers OC2 per model: A 6-man squad = 12 OC total with 24" advance range. Easily contests or captures far objectives mid-game.
  • Cronos T5/3+: Durable objective campers. With Insensible to Pain and FNP 4+ (Empowered), very hard to shift without dedicated anti-tank or high-volume fire.
